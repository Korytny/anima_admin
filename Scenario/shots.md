=== LOCATION 1: Покои Кунти ===
@time: morning
@NS: A royal chamber with carved pillars, polished stone floors, sheer curtains, a low bed, brass lamps, and soft dawn light spilling through an open window. Warm gold highlights, pale fabric, and a quiet, ceremonial atmosphere fill the room.
# Scene 1
0: #do !wide %center {}
(royal chamber at dawn, sheer curtains, polished stone floor, a bright window)
[sunlight deepens across the floor, a curtain shifts slightly in the breeze]

1: #do !medium %center {Kunti}
(Kunti stands near the window, turned toward the rising sun, her face lit by gold)
[Kunti lifts her chin, smiles faintly, and shapes a silent mantra with her lips]

2: #do !close %center {Kunti, Surya}
(Kunti stands tense as a radiant figure emerges from the sunlight before her)
[Kunti stops speaking and steps back as Surya fully appears in the glow]

# Scene 2
3: #do !medium %left {Kunti, Surya}
(Kunti stands with one arm extended toward Surya, who remains facing her in the center of the room)
[Kunti points him away, but Surya holds his ground and leans forward slightly]

4: #talk !close %right {Surya}
(Surya stands in the same light, his gaze fixed on Kunti)
[Surya breathes once, jaw tightening, then tilts his head a fraction]
*Surya: Leave empty-handed, and I burn your father and the sage ~ threatening

5: #do !medium %left {Kunti, Surya}
(Kunti stands with her arm lowered partway, Surya still motionless before her)
[Kunti slowly lowers her hand while keeping her eyes on Surya]

# Scene 3
6: #do !medium %center {Kunti, Surya}
(Kunti raises her head to meet Surya's face, both standing close in the warm light)
[Kunti fixes her gaze on Surya, and Surya gives a single slow nod without looking away]

7: #talk !close %left {Kunti}
(Kunti faces Surya, shoulders held tight and chin lifted)
[Kunti breathes in, lips parting, then steadies her posture]
*Kunti: Only leave me a maiden ~ pleading

8: #talk !close %right {Surya}
(Surya faces Kunti in a tight close-up, his expression unwavering)
[Surya blinks once, gives a small nod, and keeps his jaw set]
*Surya: You will remain one. I promise ~ calm

# Scene 4
9: #do !medium %center {}
(sheer fabric, carved pillars, warm floor light, a bedside cloth hanging still)
[the cloth shifts slightly, and light slides across the folds]

10: #do !medium %center {Kunti, Midwife}
(Kunti stands with a rounded belly concealed under her clothing, while the Midwife steps in front of her)
[Kunti smooths her garment over her abdomen as the Midwife moves into a protective position]

# Scene 5
11: #do !close %center {Kunti, Midwife, Karna_boy}
(Kunti sits low and tense as the Midwife holds a newborn with a gold chest plate and gold earrings)
[the Midwife raises the baby higher, Kunti reaches in, and her fingers trace the edge of the golden armor]

=== LOCATION 2: Берег реки ===
@time: night
@NS: A riverbank under a dark sky with rippling water, damp earth, a small woven basket lined with wax, and faint reflected moonlight on the current. Sparse reeds, a low shoreline, and a hushed, open atmosphere define the scene.
# Scene 6
12: #do !wide %center {Kunti, Karna_boy}
(Night riverbank, dark water, a wax-lined basket near the shore, Kunti leaning over it)
[Kunti lowers the basket onto the water, keeps two fingers on its rim, and bends toward the infant]

13: #talk !close %center {Kunti}
(Kunti leans over the basket, her face close to the infant, one hand still near the rim)
[Kunti breathes steadily, her lips moving softly as she blesses the child]
*Kunti: May the one who finds you be happy ~ tender

14: #do !medium %left {Kunti, Karna_boy}
(Kunti kneels at the river’s edge beside the drifting basket)
[Kunti releases the rim, and the basket drifts away from the bank]

=== LOCATION 3: Другой берег реки ===
@time: morning
@NS: A riverbank at sunrise with wet sand, shallow water, a stranded woven basket, and pale gold light spreading across the shore. Reeds line the edge of the river, and the air feels open, bright, and quiet.
# Scene 7
